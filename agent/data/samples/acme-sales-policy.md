# Acme Sales Desk Policy v4.2

Owner: Revenue Operations. These rules bind every customer-facing
commitment a salesperson makes on a call.

## POL-01 Discounts (topic: discounts)

Any promised discount above 10% needs a written pre-approval recorded in
the deal desk tool before it can be mentioned to the customer. Approval
authority: VP of Sales.

## POL-02 Support (topic: support)

24/7 or after-hours support may only be promised on Enterprise Plan
deals. Check the SKU on the quote before making any availability
commitment.

## POL-03 Data residency (topic: data_residency)

Data location commitments beyond the signup region require a legal
sign-off entry in the compliance register before signature.

## POL-04 Uptime (topic: uptime)

Uptime commitments above 99.5% are reserved for Enterprise Plan quotes.
Any higher figure requires a signed exception from Engineering.

## POL-05 Termination (topic: termination)

Refund offers on early termination contradict the MSA; finance cannot
process them. Escalate to Legal instead of promising money back.
