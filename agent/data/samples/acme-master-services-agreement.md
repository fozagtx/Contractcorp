# Master Services Agreement

**Between:** Acme Cloud, Inc. and the Customer
**Effective date:** January 15, 2026

## 1. Services

Acme provides the cloud platform described on the signed Order Form. The
service is delivered as-is except as stated in this Agreement.

## 3. Fees and Discounts

3.1 No discount is automatic. Any discount greater than ten percent (10%)
requires prior written approval from the VP of Sales, and every discount
actually granted must be listed on the signed Order Form. Verbal discount
commitments are not binding.

3.2 Invoices are due net thirty (30) days from the invoice date. Amounts
unpaid after the due date accrue interest at one percent (1%) per month
or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower.

## 4. Support

4.1 Acme provides documentation and a support portal to all plans.

4.2 Support under the Standard Plan is available 9:00 to 17:00 local time,
Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays. 24/7 support is
available only under the Enterprise Plan.

## 6. Data Protection

6.1 Customer data is stored in the region selected at signup and is not
transferred to another region without Customer's prior written consent.

## 8. Term and Termination

8.3 Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience with thirty
(30) days written notice. Termination does not entitle Customer to a
refund of prepaid fees for the remainder of the term.

## 9. Service Levels

9.1 The service targets 99.5% monthly uptime on the Standard Plan and
99.9% monthly uptime on the Enterprise Plan. Service credits for missed
targets are capped at fifteen percent (15%) of monthly fees. Uptime
commitments above the plan target are not made.

## 10. Liability

10.1 Each party's total liability under this Agreement is capped at the
fees paid by Customer in the twelve (12) months before the event giving
rise to the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential
damages.
